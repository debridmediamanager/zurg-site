A Plex server.
On your phone.

zurg+rex means running a Plex server on your phone. zurg brings in your providers and rex serves them to every Plex app at home. It runs on your TV too and any player can open your files.

Download zurg+rex

Sign in to GitHub with your sponsor account. Then pick your device. Every link points at the newest nightly. The same app has both the phone and TV interfaces. Android 8.0 or newer is required.

Getting a 404 from GitHub?

GitHub answers 404 when the account you are signed in with has no sponsor access yet. Just sponsored? Accept the invite to the repository first. Invites go out within 15 minutes. Not a sponsor yet? Sponsor on gatekeeper and link your GitHub account there.

  • Most phones and TV boxes android-arm64-v8a.apk
  • Older phones and TV sticks android-armeabi-v7a.apk
  • Chromebooks and emulators android-x86_64.apk

Newest build

The newest file list did not load. Each link opens the release list instead. Take the newest nightly at the top and not the one marked Latest.

Open the downloaded APK and allow installation from that source. Android or Play Protect may ask you to confirm sideloading.

Usenet needs a 64-bit device. Install updates over the existing app. Uninstalling removes its private configuration.

Make it yours

  1. Open zurg+rex and sign in to GitHub. Then install an engine. The app cannot start your library without one.
  2. Add your provider accounts and test the credentials. Account names let you add more than one account at a provider.
  3. Choose the profile for your phone or TV. Smaller profiles use smaller disk caches and a lower memory target.
  4. Allow notifications and background operation. Choose whether zurg starts after reboot.
  5. Start your library. Wait for Ready before opening a player. Large accounts take longer on their first load.

Usenet connection limits apply across every app and device using the account.

Press play

Open Library and choose a file. More lets you choose another player or open the file through Android documents. The zurg root also appears in compatible Files apps and document pickers.

Open your player once and finish its setup before choosing it from Files.

For a WebDAV player on the same device add http://127.0.0.1:9999/dav/ as a network source. Status shows the current address if you change the port.

On TV use the D-pad and Select. Select a text field to edit it and press Back to return to navigation. Back returns to the previous folder. Menu or More opens release actions. Fire TV can use WebDAV even when no Files app is installed.

Repair and delete act on the entire release in its owning provider account. Delete asks for confirmation.

Watch in the Plex apps

This is your Plex server running on your phone. Once your library runs Status shows rex for the Plex apps. Choose Sign in to Plex and approve the sign-in. On a TV enter the code at plex.tv/link.

Your library then shows up under this device’s name in the official Plex apps and in Plezy. That works on this device and on the others in your home. rex converts what a player cannot play with the device’s hardware encoder.

Enter your DMM API key in Settings under Library. zurg then asks DMM what each file is and more titles get their name and poster.

Turn rex off in Settings under Connection. zurg then stops feeding it.

Add more with addons

Addons bring more to watch into rex beside your library. Open Addons. The Internet Archive addon comes installed and adds public domain films. Add a repository by its link to install others.

Each addon says which websites it can reach and an update that reaches new ones asks first. Install addons only from people you trust.

Watch elsewhere at home

In Settings turn on Expose on local network. zurg creates credentials and shows your device’s WebDAV address. Add that address and those credentials to the player on your other device.

Keep the Android host running and connected. Use a trusted network because HTTP does not encrypt playback or WebDAV credentials. Local sharing is off by default.

Keep your library available

The notification shows the server state and has a Stop action. A Quick Settings tile can also start or stop zurg.

Allow background operation during setup. Some phone manufacturers need extra battery settings. See the device background guide if playback stops with the screen off.

Settings includes a YAML editor and a local Web dashboard for advanced features. Logs can be filtered and shared. Backups contain credentials and should be stored privately. Stop the server before restoring.

Updates checks the sponsor repository for the app and the engine. They update separately. If a new nightly misbehaves on your device install the engine version that worked for you. Stop your library on Status before changing the engine.

The full guide has every step with what you should see and what to do when something goes wrong.
